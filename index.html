Questions, Answered

What is AEO and GEO — and why do they matter now?+

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structures your content so search engines can lift it directly as the answer — featured snippets, People Also Ask, voice results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) goes a step further: it positions your brand to be cited and recommended inside AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. As more buyers start with an AI answer instead of ten blue links, being the cited source is the new page one.
