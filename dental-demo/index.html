About BrightCare Dental

A Neighbourhood Practice That Treats You Like Family

BrightCare Dental is an independently owned family practice in the heart of Austin. We believe good dentistry starts with listening — we take time to explain every option, agree the plan together, and never recommend treatment you don't need.

Our clinic uses digital X-rays (lower radiation), intraoral cameras so you can see what we see, and gentle techniques that make even nervous patients comfortable.

  • Transparent prices agreed before treatment starts
  • Evening and Saturday appointments for busy families
  • Flexible payment plans for larger treatments
  • Strict sterilisation and safety protocols, every patient, every time
