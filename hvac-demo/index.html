Homeowner Tips

Keep Your System Running Longer

Simple habits that lower your energy bills and prevent the most common breakdowns we see.

Every Month

Change Your Filter

A clogged filter is the #1 cause of weak airflow and frozen coils. Check it monthly and replace it every 1–3 months — more often if you have pets.

Summer

Set It and Save

Set your thermostat to 78°F when you're home and a few degrees higher when you're away. A smart thermostat can cut cooling costs without sacrificing comfort.

Winter

Book a Heating Check

Have your furnace inspected before the first cold snap. A tune-up catches worn parts early and includes a carbon-monoxide safety check for your family.
